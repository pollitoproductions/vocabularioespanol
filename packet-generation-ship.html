Activities from Generation Ship: ARK-7 — the spinning station, its six stations, the six repositories, mining, and flight rules. Print this packet and work on paper.

Name: Date:

The ARK-7 is a spinning station on its way to a new star. It stopped to mine in an asteroid field, but there was an accident, and it lost its Spanish repositories — the collections of words, cognates, idioms, verbs, dictation, and ser-versus-estar that the crew needs. Your quest is to rebuild them: speak at the stations in the agriculture ring, recover words and exercises floating in space, and mine asteroids for WATER and METAL. Guidance only, never rescue: the ship warns you at 25 and 40 km, and at 50 km you are LOST — but a lost flight is a free retry with your haul kept.

4. Mining math

All 40 asteroids can be mined. Mining them all gives 20 WATER and 570 METAL. The first crash is free; every later crash repair costs 10 WATER and 10 METAL.

  1. You have mined all 40 asteroids and hold 20 WATER. A repair costs 10 WATER. How many paid repairs can you afford?
  2. You hold 570 METAL. A repair costs 10 METAL. How many paid repairs can you afford?
  3. Your first crash was free. Your second and third crashes each need a repair. How much WATER and METAL do both repairs cost together?
  4. Beacon warnings sound at 25 km and 40 km, and you are LOST at 50 km. You are flying at 30 km. Which warning have you already passed?
  5. You hold the full haul: 20 WATER and 570 METAL. After 2 paid repairs, how much WATER and METAL do you have left?
  6. Repairs are limited by whichever resource runs out first. From the full haul, how many paid repairs can you afford in total, and which resource limits you?
  7. You are flying at 45 km. Which warnings have you passed, and what happens if you reach 50 km?
  8. A repair costs 10 WATER and 10 METAL. You hold 5 WATER and 570 METAL. Can you afford a repair? Why or why not?
