1. Match the real meaning

1 = l; 2 = k; 3 = j; 4 = i; 5 = h; 6 = g; 7 = f; 8 = e; 9 = d; 10 = c; 11 = b; 12 = a

2. The trap — Spanish to English

1. lectura = reading (not “lecture”) — Me gusta la lectura.
2. sensible = sensitive (not “sensible”) — Es muy sensible al frío.
3. constipado = having a cold (not “constipated”) — Estoy constipado y toso mucho.
4. carpeta = folder (not “carpet”) — Guarda los papeles en la carpeta.
5. actual = current (not “actual”) — El mes actual es noviembre.
6. advertir = to warn (not “to advertise”) — Te advierto del peligro.
7. balón = ball (not “balloon”) — El balón es de fútbol.
8. campo = countryside (not “camp”) — El campo es verde.
9. casualidad = coincidence (not “casualty”) — Fue una casualidad, no un plan.
10. conductor = driver (not “conductor”) — El conductor maneja el autobús.
11. contestar = to answer (not “to contest”) — Contesta la pregunta, por favor.
12. decepción = disappointment (not “deception”) — Qué decepción, no ganó el premio.

3. Write the real meaning — Spanish to English

<b>desgracia</b> — Qué desgracia, se rompió la pierna. = misfortune; <b>tarjeta</b> — La tarjeta es azul. = card; <b>fábrica</b> — Trabaja en una fábrica de zapatos. = factory; <b>firma</b> — Escribe tu firma aquí. = signature; <b>globo</b> — El globo flota en el aire. = balloon; <b>introducir</b> — Introduce la llave en la cerradura. = to insert; <b>noticia</b> — Veo las noticias cada noche. = news; <b>pan</b> — Como pan todos los días. = bread; <b>pariente</b> — Es pariente lejano. = relative; <b>profesión</b> — ¿Cuál es tu profesión? = profession; <b>quitar</b> — Quita la mesa, por favor. = to remove; <b>receta</b> — Sigue la receta para hacer el pan. = recipe

4. Match the real meaning — English to Spanish

1 = l; 2 = k; 3 = j; 4 = i; 5 = h; 6 = g; 7 = f; 8 = e; 9 = d; 10 = c; 11 = b; 12 = a

5. The trap — English to Spanish

1. lecture = conferencia (not “lectura”) — The lecture was very long.
2. sensible = sensato (not “sensible”) — That was a sensible decision.
3. constipated = estreñido (not “constipado”) — He is constipated and needs medicine.
4. carpet = alfombra (not “carpeta”) — The carpet is red.
5. actual = real (not “actual”) — The actual price is higher.
6. advertise = anunciar (not “advertir”) — They advertise the new phone on TV.
7. balloon = globo (not “balón”) — The balloon floats in the air.
8. camp = acampar (not “campo”) — We camp in the mountains.
9. casualty = víctima (not “casualidad”) — There was one casualty in the accident.
10. conductor = material conductor (not “conductor”) — Copper is a good conductor.
11. contest = impugnar (not “contestar”) — He will contest the decision.
12. deception = engaño (not “decepción”) — The deception was discovered.

6. Write the real meaning — English to Spanish

<b>disgrace</b> — It was a disgrace to lose like that. = vergüenza; <b>target</b> — Hit the target in the center. = objetivo; <b>fabric</b> — This shirt is made of soft fabric. = tela; <b>firm</b> — She works for a big firm. = empresa; <b>globe</b> — The globe shows the whole world. = globo terráqueo; <b>introduce</b> — Let me introduce my friend Ana. = presentar; <b>notice</b> — Did you see the notice on the door? = aviso; <b>pan</b> — Cook the eggs in the pan. = sartén; <b>parent</b> — My parent is a teacher. = padre o madre; <b>professor</b> — The professor teaches history. = profesor universitario; <b>quit</b> — He decided to quit his job. = renunciar; <b>receipt</b> — Keep the receipt for the shoes. = recibo

7. Use them in sentences

1. Model: La red no funciona hoy.
2. Model: Me pongo la ropa.
3. Model: Es sano porque hace ejercicio.
4. Model: Es simpático y hace reír a todos.
5. Model: El suceso ocurrió ayer.
6. Model: Un vaso de agua, por favor.

8. Review quiz

1. taller = workshop (not “taller”)
2. bizarro = brave (not “bizarre”)
3. suceder = to happen (not “to succeed”)
4. reunión = meeting (not “reunion”)
5. retirar = to withdraw (not “to retire”)
6. red = rojo (not “red”)
7. rope = cuerda (not “ropa”)
8. sane = cuerdo (not “sano”)
9. sympathetic = compasiva (not “simpático”)
10. success = éxito (not “suceso”)

9. Reflection

Open answers — any thoughtful response counts.

For the teacher

Purpose: the first paper practice for the False friends tool, in both directions — the trap works both ways, so the packet practices both. Timing: about 40 minutes. Tip: do not let students guess from the English lookalike; insist they learn each word’s real meaning, and use the model sentences in exercise 7 to check their own sentences.
