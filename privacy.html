Privacy Policy

Vocabulario Español is a free browser game for learning Spanish vocabulary.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or account. Your progress and settings (such as sound volume) are saved only in your own browser using local storage. This data never leaves your device.

Voice features

Some tools can speak Spanish words aloud using your browser's built-in text-to-speech. The words spoken are the learning content itself. Depending on your browser, this may briefly send the word to your browser maker's speech service; we do not receive or store it.

Some tools also let students say a word or phrase aloud to check their pronunciation. The microphone only turns on when the student taps the microphone button — it is never listening in the background — and speaking is always optional: it never blocks learning or progress. When a student speaks, their voice is sent to their browser maker's speech service so it can be transcribed and checked. We do not record or store voice recordings, and no voice audio is sent to us.

This website may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, which may be personalized based on your visits to this and other sites. Ad placement and frequency are controlled by Google AdSense.

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com. See also how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland may be shown a consent choice before personalized ads are served.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
