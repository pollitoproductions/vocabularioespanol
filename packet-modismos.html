An idiom (modismo) is a phrase whose meaning is not the sum of its words. Translated word by word, most idioms sound strange or wrong — that is exactly why they are worth learning. The 135 idioms in this packet come from everyday Spanish across the Spanish-speaking world, so they are useful anywhere Spanish is spoken.
