El Glosario Perdido

You are the keeper of a great glosario, a book holding every Spanish word a traveler could need. One stormy night, its pages came loose and scattered across a strange, quiet planet.

Each lost page became a glowing vocabulary card, hidden among the trees, shores, and hills. Without them, the glossary stays empty, and the words are forgotten.

Explore the planet, find every card, and read each word aloud as you collect it. Every card is pronounced for you as you pick it up — listen, then repeat it out loud. Only when all 1,000 cards are back in your book will the glosario be complete.

We do not ask for your name, email, or account. Your progress and settings (such as sound volume) are saved only in your own browser using local storage. This data never leaves your device.
