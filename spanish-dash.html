How to play

Tap, click, or press Space to jump. Dodge spikes, blocks, and gaps — land on the orange platforms to hop over trouble. At each checkpoint, pick the right answer from 4 choices to keep running. Pick the wrong answer (except in the Cognates levels) and you go back to the last checkpoint. Speaking is optional — turn on Speaking activities on the home menu if you also want to say each answer out loud. If the game mishears you when you speak, just try again as many times as you like.
