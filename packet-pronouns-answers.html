1. Gustar-type verbs

1. A mí me gusta el libro. — I like the book.
2. Me gusta nadar en el verano. — I like swimming in the summer.
3. Me encanta el café. — I love coffee, it's my favorite drink.
4. Me fascina muchísimo la música clásica. — Classical music really fascinates me.
5. Me interesa la historia de México. — I'm interested in the history of Mexico, I want to learn more.
6. A mí me molesta el ruido. — The street noise bothers me.
7. Me duele la cabeza hoy. — My head hurts today.
8. Me falta tiempo para estudiar. — I need time to study.
9. Me gustas mucho. — I like you a lot, you're very kind.
10. Me gustan los libros de aventuras. — I like adventure books.

2. Direct objects

1. Lo veo todos los días. — I see him every day, he's my brother.
2. Lo conozco bien. — I know him well, he's the Spanish teacher.
3. ¿Lo tienes? — Do you have it? The book is new.
4. Lo compro en el mercado. — I buy it at the market, the bread is fresh.
5. María lo lee cada noche. — María reads it every night, the book is very good.
6. Lo busco en mi mochila. — I'm looking for it in my backpack, the pencil is blue.
7. Lo llamo por teléfono. — I call him on the phone, my father is at home.
8. Lo ayudo con la tarea. — I help him with the homework, my brother doesn't understand.
9. Lo visito en el hospital. — I visit him in the hospital, my grandfather is sick.
10. Lo escucho en la radio. — I listen to it on the radio, the program is fun.

3. Reflexive pronouns

1. Yo me levanto a las siete. — I get up at seven.
2. Me lavo las manos antes de comer. — I wash my hands before eating.
3. Yo me visto rápido por la mañana. — I get dressed quickly in the morning.
4. Yo me ducho por la mañana. — I shower in the morning.
5. Yo me siento aquí. — I sit down here, near the window.
6. Me acuesto. — I go to bed at ten at night.
7. Me despierto todos los días. — I wake up at six every day.
8. Me peino el pelo antes de salir. — I comb my hair before going out.
9. Me cepillo los dientes después de comer. — I brush my teeth after eating.
10. Yo me llamo Ana, ¿y tú? — My name is Ana, and you?

4. Indirect objects

1. Le doy el libro a Ana. — I give the book to Ana.
2. Le digo la verdad a Carlos. — I tell Carlos the truth.
3. Le escribo una carta a María. — I write a letter to María.
4. Le compro un regalo a mi madre. — I buy a gift for my mother.
5. Le cuento una historia a Luis. — I tell Luis a story.
6. Le explico la lección a Pedro. — I explain the lesson to Pedro.
7. Le muestro la foto a Juan. — I show Juan the photo.
8. Le regalo un libro a Ana. — I give Ana a book as a gift.

5. After prepositions

1. Este regalo es para mí. — This gift is for me, it's my birthday today.
2. No te vayas sin mí. — Don't go without me, I can't go alone.
3. Hablan de mí siempre. — They always talk about me, I'm famous at school.
4. Llaman a mí por teléfono. — They call me on the phone, I have the number.
5. Piensan en mí mucho. — They think about me a lot, I'm their favorite son.
6. Lo hacen por mí. — They do it for me, it's my birthday party.
7. Este café es para mí. — This coffee is for me, I ordered it.
8. No salgas sin mí. — Don't go out without me, I have the keys.

6. Possessives

1. El libro es mío. — I bought the book, the book is mine.
2. El coche es mío. — The car is mine, I paid for it.
3. Yo digo que el lápiz es mío. — I say the pencil is mine.
4. El teléfono es mío. — The phone is mine, I use it every day.
5. Este sombrero es mío. — This hat is mine, I bought it in Mexico.
6. El reloj es mío. — The watch is mine, my grandfather gave it to me.
7. Yo creo que el cuaderno es mío. — I think the notebook is mine.
8. La casa es mía. — The house is mine, I live here.

7. Demonstratives

1. Este libro aquí es muy interesante. — This book here in my hand is very interesting.
2. Mira, este coche aquí es nuevo. — Look, this car here is new.
3. Este lápiz aquí escribe muy bien. — This pencil here writes very well.
4. Este teléfono aquí es mío. — This phone here in my pocket is mine.
5. ¿Este libro aquí es tuyo o mío? — Is this book here yours or mine?
6. Este café aquí está caliente. — This coffee here is hot.
7. Esta computadora aquí es rápida. — This computer here on my desk is fast.
8. Este perro aquí es muy tranquilo. — This dog here near me is very calm.

9. Review quiz

1. A mí me fascinan las montañas. — The mountains in winter fascinate me.
2. Me interesan los idiomas. — I'm interested in languages, I want to learn three.
3. Lo quiero mucho. — I love him a lot, my dog is my best friend.
4. Lo necesito para la clase. — I need it for class, the notebook is new.
5. Me pongo el abrigo. — I put on the coat because it is cold.
6. Le preparo la cena a Pedro. — I prepare dinner for Pedro.
7. Escriben a mí todos los días. — They write to me every day, I'm their friend.
8. La bicicleta es mía. — The bike is mine, I use it to go to school.
9. Esta casa aquí es muy grande. — This house here near me is very big.
10. Esta mesa aquí es nueva. — This table here is new.

10. Reflection

Open answers — any thoughtful response counts.

For the teacher

Purpose: the first paper practice for the Pronouns tool — all seven families in one packet, so students see how they fit together. Timing: about 45 minutes, or split after exercise 4 (exercises 1–4, then 5–9). Tip: the most common error is agreement — drill that gustar agrees with the thing liked and possessives agree with the thing owned, not the owner.
