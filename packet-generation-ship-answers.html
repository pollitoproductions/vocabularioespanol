Answer key: Generation Ship packet

Answers for the Generation Ship packet packet. Teachers only — the packet page itself has no answers on it.

← Back to the packet

1. Quest order

Recover the nearest word or exercise floating in space. = 4; Visit the Cognates station in the agriculture ring. = 1; Open the in-game menu and review your progress (PROGRESS, then REVIEW EVERYTHING). = 6; Speak 3 cognates at the station. = 2; Return aboard the ship. = 5; Depart the ship in your shuttle. = 3

2. Repository answers

Vocabulary = f; Cognates = e; Idioms = d; Verbs = c; Dictation = b; Ser vs. estar = a

3. Station answers

Cognates station = Cognates; Conjugation station = Verbs; Idioms station = Idioms; Dictation station = Dictation; Ser vs. estar station = Ser vs. estar. Station with no matching repository: Practice.

4. Mining math answers

1. 2 paid repairs (20 ÷ 10). 2. 57 paid repairs (570 ÷ 10). 3. 20 WATER and 20 METAL (2 × 10 each). 4. The 25 km warning (30 km is past 25, not yet 40). 5. 0 WATER and 550 METAL. 6. 2 paid repairs, limited by WATER (20 ÷ 10 = 2, before METAL runs out). 7. Both warnings passed (25 and 40); at 50 km you are LOST, a free retry with your haul kept. 8. No — a repair needs 10 WATER and you hold only 5.

5. True or false answers

1. T; 2. F; 3. T; 4. T; 5. F; 6. T; 7. T; 8. T; 9. T; 10. T — Flying past 50 km is a free retry with your haul kept; stations open with the cyan LEARN button within 8 m, not by walking past.

6. Stations

Cognates, Practice, Conjugation, Idioms, Dictation, Ser vs. estar (any order).

7. Plan a voyage

Open answers — any correct retelling in order counts.

8. Voyage log

Open answers — records a real play session.

9. Reflection

Open answers — any thoughtful response counts.

For the teacher

Purpose: a paper companion to a class visit to the ship — or a way in for students who only read about the game. Timing: about 40 minutes. Tip: exercise 4 is the real math of the game (totals, limits, remainders); exercise 3 catches the common mistake of assuming every station feeds a repository.
