How to play

Walk around the world, find a floating vocabulary card, and collect it. The controls are below for keyboard and for touch screens.

Controls

WASD Move
↑↓←→ Look
SPACE Acción / Action / Collect
ENTER Levitar / Levitate
F Correr / Sprint
M Menu
📱 Touch screens: use the left side to move and the right side to look.

Collecting cards

Walk up to a card and use the action button (or the Space bar) to collect it. The card is added to your inventory in the menu.

Sprint and levitate

Sprint (Correr) lets you cover ground faster, and levitate (Levitar) lifts you off the ground. Both use stamina, which recovers when you stop using them, and they can’t be used at the same time.

Menu and saving

Open the menu with the MENU button or the M key to review your inventory and change the sound volume. Your progress is saved in your browser, so use Continue on the start screen to return later.
