Answer key: Tú commands packet

Answers for the Tú commands packet packet. Teachers only — the packet page itself has no answers on it.

← Back to the packet

1. Affirmative — the 8 irregulars

1. haz la tarea ahora. — Do the homework now.
2. ve a la escuela. — Go to school.
3. di la verdad, por favor. — Tell the truth, please.
4. pon el libro aquí. — Put the book here.
5. sal de la casa. — Leave the house.
6. sé bueno con tu hermana. — Be nice to your sister.
7. ten paciencia, por favor. — Have patience, please.
8. ven a mi casa. — Come to my house.

2. Affirmative — regular -ar verbs

1. habla con tu profesor. — Talk to your teacher.
2. mira la foto. — Look at the photo.
3. escucha la música. — Listen to the music.
4. estudia español hoy. — Study Spanish today.
5. trabaja en la tienda. — Work at the store.
6. limpia tu cuarto. — Clean your room.
7. llama a tu madre. — Call your mom.
8. espera un momento. — Wait a moment.
9. busca tus llaves. — Look for your keys.
10. compra pan, por favor. — Buy bread, please.

3. Affirmative — -er / -ir verbs and stem-changers

1. lee el libro, por favor. — Read the book, please.
2. come tu cena. — Eat your dinner.
3. aprende las palabras. — Learn the words.
4. bebe agua. — Drink water.
5. corre a la meta. — Run to the finish line.
6. escribe tu nombre. — Write your name.
7. abre la ventana. — Open the window.
8. cierra la puerta, por favor. — Close the door, please.
9. empieza ahora mismo. — Start right now.
10. piensa en la respuesta. — Think about the answer.

4. Negative — the 8 irregulars

1. No digas mentiras. — Don't tell lies.
2. No hagas eso. — Don't do that.
3. No vayas a la fiesta. — Don't go to the party.
4. No pongas eso aquí. — Don't put that here.
5. No salgas de noche. — Don't go out at night.
6. No seas malo. — Don't be mean.
7. No tengas miedo. — Don't be afraid.
8. No vengas tarde. — Don't come late.

5. Negative — regular verbs

1. No hables en clase. — Don't talk in class.
2. No mires la tele. — Don't watch TV.
3. No escuches esa música. — Don't listen to that music.
4. No estudies tan tarde. — Don't study so late.
5. No trabajes hoy. — Don't work today.
6. No limpies la mesa. — Don't clean the table.
7. No llames a Juan. — Don't call Juan.
8. No esperes más. — Don't wait anymore.
9. No busques mis llaves. — Don't look for my keys.
10. No compres ese libro. — Don't buy that book.

6. Affirmative or negative?

1. prepara la cena. — Make dinner.
2. No prepares la cena. — Don't make dinner.
3. sirve la sopa. — Serve the soup.
4. No sirvas la sopa. — Don't serve the soup.
5. saca la basura. — Take out the trash.
6. No saques la basura. — Don't take out the trash.
7. apaga la luz. — Turn off the light.
8. No apagues la luz. — Don't turn off the light.
9. enciende la lámpara. — Turn on the lamp.
10. No enciendas la lámpara. — Don't turn on the lamp.

7. Pronoun placement

1. hazlo ahora, por favor. — Do it now, please.
2. dime por favor. — Tell me, please.
3. ponlo aquí. — Put it here.
4. No lo hagas ahora, por favor. — Don't do it now, please.
5. No me digas nada. — Don't tell me anything.

8. Write it from memory

1. canta una canción. — Sing a song.
2. baila conmigo. — Dance with me.
3. ayuda a tu amigo. — Help your friend.
4. pregunta al profesor. — Ask the teacher.
5. contesta la pregunta. — Answer the question.
6. termina tu tarea. — Finish your homework.
7. duerme ocho horas. — Sleep eight hours.
8. pide ayuda, por favor. — Ask for help, please.

9. Review quiz

1. sigue las instrucciones. — Follow the instructions.
2. juega al fútbol. — Play soccer.
3. cuenta hasta diez. — Count to ten.
4. da el libro a Ana. — Give the book to Ana.
5. lava los platos. — Wash the dishes.
6. barre el piso. — Sweep the floor.
7. No cierres la puerta. — Don't close the door.
8. No cantes ahora. — Don't sing now.
9. No bailes aquí. — Don't dance here.
10. No ayudes a tu hermano. — Don't help your brother.

10. Reflection

Open answers — any thoughtful response counts.

For the teacher

Purpose: paper practice for Tú commands, built for a Spanish 2 review — affirmative and negative forms side by side, including all eight irregulars in both forms. Timing: about 40 minutes. Tip: have students chant the eight irregulars (di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé, ten, ven) before exercise 1, and point out that the word “no” is the only clue they need in exercise 6.
