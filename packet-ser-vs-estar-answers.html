Answer key: Ser vs. estar packet

Answers for the Ser vs. estar packet packet. Teachers only — the packet page itself has no answers on it.

← Back to the packet

1. Set 1 answers

Yo ___ profesor. = ser (Ser: profession; I am a teacher.); Yo ___ en casa. = estar (Estar: location; I am at home.); Yo ___ de Argentina. = ser (Ser: origin; I am from Argentina.); Yo ___ cansado. = estar (Estar: condition; I am tired.); Yo ___ alto. = ser (Ser: physical trait; I am tall.); Yo ___ feliz. = estar (Estar: emotion; I am happy.); Yo ___ el hermano de Ana. = ser (Ser: identity; I am Ana's brother.); Yo ___ comiendo pan. = estar (Estar: progressive; I am eating bread.); Yo ___ joven. = ser (Ser: trait; I am young.); Yo ___ enfermo. = estar (Estar: condition; I am sick.); Yo ___ estudiante. = ser (Ser: identity; I am a student.); Yo ___ en la escuela. = estar (Estar: location; I am at school.); Yo ___ de Chile. = ser (Ser: origin; I am from Chile.); Yo ___ listo para salir. = estar (Estar: ready; I am ready to go out.); Yo ___ fuerte. = ser (Ser: trait; I am strong.); Yo ___ de pie. = estar (Estar: position; I am standing.)

2. Set 2 answers

Yo ___ médico. = ser (Ser: profession; I am a doctor.); Yo ___ contento con el libro. = estar (Estar: emotion; I am happy with the book.); Yo ___ el dueño del perro. = ser (Ser: possession; I am the dog's owner.); Yo ___ en el jardín. = estar (Estar: location; I am in the garden.); Tú ___ muy simpático. = ser (Ser: character trait; You are very nice.); Tú ___ en la tienda. = estar (Estar: location; You are at the store.); Tú ___ de Colombia. = ser (Ser: origin; You are from Colombia.); Tú ___ enfermo. = estar (Estar: condition; You are sick.); Tú ___ estudiante. = ser (Ser: identity; You are a student.); Tú ___ muy feliz. = estar (Estar: emotion; You are very happy.); Tú ___ alto. = ser (Ser: physical trait; You are tall.); Tú ___ comiendo pizza. = estar (Estar: progressive; You are eating pizza.); Tú ___ mi hermano. = ser (Ser: identity; You are my brother.); Tú ___ cansado. = estar (Estar: condition; You are tired.); Tú ___ joven. = ser (Ser: trait; You are young.); Tú ___ en casa. = estar (Estar: location; You are at home.)

3. Fill + rule answers

Tú ___ el profesor de Ana. = ser, rule a (Ser: profession); Tú ___ listo para la escuela. = estar, rule b (Estar: ready); Tú ___ amable. = ser, rule c (Ser: character trait); Tú ___ triste. = estar, rule d (Estar: emotion); Tú ___ el dueño del gato. = ser, rule e (Ser: possession); Tú ___ en el hospital. = estar, rule f (Estar: location); Tú ___ fuerte. = ser, rule g (Ser: trait); Tú ___ nervioso. = estar, rule d (Estar: emotion); Ella ___ profesora. = ser, rule a (Ser: profession); Mi madre ___ en casa. = estar, rule f (Estar: location); Él ___ de Perú. = ser, rule h (Ser: origin); Ella ___ enferma. = estar, rule i (Estar: condition)

4. Rule answers

La casa ___ de madera. = a (Ser: material); ___ la una. = b (Ser: time); La película ___ a las tres. = c (Ser: event time); Él ___ aburrido; nadie quiere hablar con él. = d (Tricky pair: ser = a boring person); Ella ___ comiendo arroz. = e (Estar: progressive); La puerta ___ cerrada. = f (Estar: resulting state); El perro ___ dormido. = g (Estar: condition); Él ___ aburrido en clase. = h (Tricky pair: estar = bored right now)

5. Write your own

Open answers — any correct sentence for the prompt counts.

6. Review quiz answers

Mi abuela ___ muy buena con los niños. = ser (Tricky pair: ser = a kind person; My grandmother is very good with children.); La sopa ___ muy buena. = estar (Tricky pair: estar = tastes good; The soup tastes very good.); Él ___ muy listo; todo lo entiende. = ser (Tricky pair: ser = smart; He is very smart; he understands everything.); Él ___ listo para salir. = estar (Tricky pair: estar = ready; He is ready to go out.); No me gusta Luis; ___ muy malo con todos. = ser (Tricky pair: ser = mean; I don't like Luis; he is very mean to everyone.); María no viene a clase porque ___ mala. = estar (Tricky pair: estar = sick; María isn't coming to class because she is sick.); Él ___ rico; tiene tres casas. = ser (Tricky pair: ser = rich; He is rich; he has three houses.); La pizza ___ rica. = estar (Tricky pair: estar = tastes good; The pizza tastes good.); Nosotros ___ estudiantes. = ser (Ser: identity; We are students.); Nosotros ___ en la escuela. = estar (Estar: location; We are at school.)

7. Reflection

Open answers — any thoughtful response counts.

For the teacher

Purpose: the packet version of the Ser vs. estar tool — same 100 questions, now as paper practice with the rule named every time. Timing: about 45 minutes, or split across two days (exercises 1–3, then 4–7). Tip: exercise 3 is the heart of it — students who can name the rule stop guessing. The reflection paragraph makes a good exit ticket.
