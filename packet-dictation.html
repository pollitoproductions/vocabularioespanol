Dictation companion sheet

Use this sheet with the Dictation tool. Your teacher plays a word, phrase, or verb form from the Vocab, Verbs, or Modismos tab — listen, then write exactly what you heard on a numbered line. There is no answer key for this sheet: the answers are the words that were played.

The tool has three tabs. The Vocab tab plays words from the 1,000-word vocabulary list — nouns with their articles, verbs, and other everyday words. The Verbs tab plays verb forms: listen for the tense as well as the verb, because the same verb can sound almost the same in two tenses. The Modismos tab plays whole idioms — write the full phrase, not just one word. This sheet gives you room for a full round on each tab, plus sorting and speaking practice to do after you listen.

Dictation is listening practice: the ear has to catch the word before the hand can write it. Work one set at a time. Listen to the whole word first, repeat it silently, then write. If you miss one, leave it blank and catch the next — you can return to it in the retry round.

How a round works: your teacher plays each word twice, with a short pause between words. Do not start writing on the first sound — hear the whole word, then write. Spelling counts, including accents: hablo and habló are two different words, and the accent is the only thing that tells them apart.

E. Listen for the accent

Some Spanish words differ by one accent — and the accent changes the stress, the meaning, and the tense. Your teacher plays one word from each pair below. Write the word you heard on the line.

Tip: in each pair the first word is the present-tense yo form (I …) and the second is the preterite él/ella form (he/she …). The accent marks the stressed syllable — listen for it.

  1. hablo (I speak) / habló (he/she spoke) — I heard:
  2. canto (I sing) / cantó (he/she sang) — I heard:
  3. bailo (I dance) / bailó (he/she danced) — I heard:
  4. cocino (I cook) / cocinó (he/she cooked) — I heard:
  5. miro (I look) / miró (he/she looked) — I heard:
  6. trabajo (I work) / trabajó (he/she worked) — I heard:
  7. estudio (I study) / estudió (he/she studied) — I heard:
  8. como (I eat) / comió (he/she ate) — I heard:
  9. bebo (I drink) / bebió (he/she drank) — I heard:
  10. aprendo (I learn) / aprendió (he/she learned) — I heard:

J. Reflection

Answer in English or Spanish.
