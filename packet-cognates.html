A cognate is a word that looks and means almost the same in Spanish and English, because both languages inherited it from Latin. That means you already know hundreds of Spanish words before your first lesson. There are three kinds: perfect cognates (the same word, like animal), pattern cognates (the same pattern with a small spelling change, like información / information), and verb cognates (like abandonar / to abandon). Learn to spot the patterns and your vocabulary doubles fast.
