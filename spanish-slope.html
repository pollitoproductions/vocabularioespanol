How to play

Your ball rolls downhill on its own, faster and faster. Steer left and right to dodge the red blocks and the gaps in the track. If you roll off a gap or hit a block, you crash.

Some stretches of track tilt to the left or the right — the slope pulls your ball toward the low side, so steer against the tilt to hold your line. Gaps on a tilted stretch are jumped the same way.

Neon rings mark a tunnel — just scenery, roll straight through. And watch the edges: the ball can fall off the side of the track now, so don't ride the edge to dodge.

Amber pads with chevrons are launch ramps — roll over one and it flings you into the air, across a wide gap or up onto a raised platform. Just ride onto it; no tap needed.

Hop: tap, press Space, or press ↑ to hop — a big bounce that clears a block or a gap. You can hop about once every half second, so time it.

Glowing word tiles float over the track — roll through one and the game pauses so you can say the word out loud. Say it correctly and you earn a free revive: the next time you crash you revive instantly with no quiz. Free revives stack — say more words, bank more skips. Don't want to speak it? Tap “Keep rolling” to skip — skipping earns nothing.

When you crash, you get a quiz revive: 5 Spanish questions. Get 4 or 5 right and you keep rolling, with a short head start and 2 seconds of flashing invincibility. You can revive as many times as you like. The questions follow the same order as Spanish Dash — cognates, then common words, then present tense, then possessive pronouns, then ser vs. estar — ten revives of each, and then they start over, so there are 50 different revives.

Keyboard: ← → or A / D to steer, Space or ↑ to hop, P or Esc to pause, M to mute.
Touch: drag left or right to steer, tap to hop.
