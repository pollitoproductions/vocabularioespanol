How to play

Your ball rolls downhill on its own, faster and faster. Steer left and right to dodge the red blocks and the gaps in the track. If you roll off a gap or hit a block, you crash.

Glowing word tiles float over the track — roll through one to collect it (+10 points) and see what it means.

When you crash, you get a quiz revive: 5 Spanish questions. Get 4 or 5 right and you keep rolling, with a short head start and 2 seconds of flashing invincibility. You get 3 revives per run. The questions follow the same order as Spanish Dash — cognates, then practice words, then present tense, then possessive pronouns, then ser vs. estar — and then they start over, so there are 50 different revives.

Keyboard: ← → or A / D to steer, P or Esc to pause, M to mute.
Touch: drag left or right to steer.
