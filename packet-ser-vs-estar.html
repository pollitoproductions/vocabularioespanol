Ser vs. estar packet

Exercises from the Ser vs. estar tool (100 questions). Print this packet and work on paper.

Name: Date:

Spanish has two verbs for “to be.” Ser is for identity and lasting traits: what something is, where someone is from, what time it is, what something is made of. Estar is for states and place: how someone feels, where something is right now, how something looks at the moment. The same adjective can change meaning with the verb — that is the whole game, and this packet trains it.

7. Reflection
