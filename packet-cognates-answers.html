9. Reflection

Open answers — any thoughtful response counts.

For the teacher

Purpose: a first-week confidence builder — students discover they already know hundreds of Spanish words. Timing: about 40 minutes, or split across two days (exercises 1–4, then 5–8). Tip: let students spot the patterns themselves before naming them; the -ción pattern in exercise 6 is the one they will meet most.
