LEARNING STATIONS
The seven tool stations are walk-up consoles in the agriculture ring: walk up to a glowing ring and tap LEARN (or press Enter); Close or Escape returns you to walking. Every station speaks in Spanish and English and lets you practise saying it (ding on a correct answer). A station never blocks you — speaking is feedback only, except that spoken Spanish earns progress and +5 WATER / +5 METAL, the same reward as answering that question out in space. On Practice, Conjugation, Ser vs. estar, and Saber vs. conocer, the audio row stays hidden until you answer — answer first, then listen.
COLLECTING IN SPACE
Fly within 250 m of a floating word or exercise question to pause and pronounce it: words ask for the Spanish word; questions ask you to speak the answer (tap Show answer to peek — peeking never counts, only speaking does). Say it right to collect it for +5 WATER and +5 METAL; Leave (or Escape) flies on, and the item waits until you are 400 m clear. A gold arrow points to the nearest uncollected item — word or question — with a tag saying which; distant items show as small glow dots until their plates stream in.
REVIEW TERMINALS
Eight gold review terminals stand around the industrial side — one on the mezzanine, seven on the main deck, spread evenly around the hull. Walk up and tap Review (or press Enter) to review everything you have already spoken, by tool, with Hear in both languages. Reviewing never changes progress.
DEPOSITORIES
Eight depositories rebuild the ship’s city: four blue WATER depositories in the agriculture ring and four amber METAL depositories in the residential half. Walk up and tap DEPOSIT (or press Enter) to deposit material from your haul in person — 250 WATER regrows one crop bed in the farm, 250 METAL rebuilds one residential block (and its little gardens appear too, planted). Partial deposits bank toward the next build, so you can deposit what you have and come back later. Crop beds start ungrown and residential blocks start unbuilt — they appear only when you regrow or rebuild them.
QUESTS
Your quest is to rebuild the lost Spanish repositories — vocabulary, cognates, idioms, verbs, dictation, ser vs. estar, saber vs. conocer, and pronouns. The quest box (top left) shows what you have collected in each repository, with an arrow pointing to your next tutorial step, and celebrates each repository you finish — and all of them together. Quests guide; they never block — skip the tutorial anytime from the quest box.
SAVING
SAVE JOURNEY in this menu saves at once; the game also saves after every collect, every mining yield, and every docking. GO TO HOME SCREEN saves, then returns to the title screen. Resuming restores your haul, your collected items, and your position — aboard or in flight.
An ark bound for another star. Spin gravity holds the decks — thrust is the only other gravity you’ll get. Nine glowing docking ports: eight around the hull, one at the top end-cap hub. Fly within 100 m of any port and you dock automatically — there is no dock button. Hitting the hull or an asteroid anywhere else ends the run. An asteroid belt drifts clear of the ship — dodge the rocks; any asteroid can be mined (hold M, or the MINE button, for about 3 seconds within 100 m) for water from icy comets and metal from metal rocks. Jumps inside the ship: the ground slides beneath you while you’re airborne, because the ship keeps spinning and you don’t. (Jump arcs feel Mars-like by movement tuning; the ship’s spin gravity itself is unchanged.) After departing, docking re-arms once you fly 150 m clear of every port, so launching never snaps you straight back aboard. Inside, the ship is divided into sections: AGRICULTURE aft (open farmland) and the enclosed industrial half forward, split into TECHNOLOGY (plus a two-story gallery hall with stair towers and a railed upper gallery) and EDUCATION; a bulkhead with four glowing entrances divides the halves. Beacon range: a warning appears past 25 km from the ship, grows urgent past 40 km, and past 50 km you are LOST and the run ends (retry returns you aboard, haul kept, free). The star is huge and sits about 30 km out — fly within 5 km of it and your heat shield vaporizes, ending the run. A cyan beacon beam rises from the nearest port while you fly, and the nav readout adds an up/down elevation angle, so guidance works in 3D — but it only ever points, it never steers or returns you. Farther out, all 1,000 Spanish vocabulary words float from about 3 km to 20 km out, plus exercise questions between 1.5 km and 15 km out — fly within 250 m of one to pause and pronounce it; say it right to collect it for +5 WATER and +5 METAL. A gold arrow under the port readout points to the nearest uncollected item — word or exercise question — with a tag saying which, and distant items show as small glow dots until their plates stream in. Crash repairs cost 10 WATER + 10 METAL; your first crash is free. If you can't pay, you return to your last save.

GENERATION SHIP — ARK-7

The ARK-7 arrived at a new star — but there was no planet, only an asteroid belt circling it. The crew decided to mine the belt for resources before moving on to the next star — but there was an accident, and the ship lost its Spanish repositories. The second language it carried for the next generation is scattered among the rocks. Now the crew is stationed in the asteroid belt to regroup: fly out, recover every word, speak it aloud, and rebuild what was lost — so the knowledge is not lost to the next generation.
