Free classroom resources

A free printable pack to go with the games, made by a classroom teacher for his own students and shared with anyone who can use it.

The pack has two parts. The student worksheets are print-and-go pages built from real words in the game, and the teacher guide explains how to run the lessons and includes the answer keys. It is written for novice learners in grades 3 to 8 and for Spanish 1, and it can be used for a single class period or stretched into a five-day mini-unit. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay.

Student worksheets

Seventeen worksheets that send students back into the game to find and collect words, then practice them on paper. Topics include the el and la gender rules, numbers and math, countries, colors, animals, verbs, jobs, food, weather and nature, directions, and building simple sentences. There is also a scavenger hunt, a short quiz, and a reflection page for the end of a lesson.

Teacher guide and answer key

A quick-start guide to the game and its controls, lesson plans from a single period up to a five-day unit, ideas for centers and substitute days, notes on differentiation and assessment, connections to standards, a worksheet index, and the answer keys for the worksheets that have one.

Before your first lesson

Try the game on the devices your students will actually use, whether that is laptops, Chromebooks, tablets, or phones, so there are no surprises on the day. The game runs in a modern web browser, so you can put the link on the board or share it with families. If you are choosing between our two games for a class, the games page explains the difference. Backrooms has a spookier atmosphere, so most teachers will want to start with Vocabulario Español.

The Spanish learning guides are a good companion for students who want the grammar behind the words, and the vocabulary list shows every word in alphabetical order.
